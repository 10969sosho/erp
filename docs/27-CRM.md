# CRM

## Purpose and Problem

Menyatukan prospek, customer relationship, opportunity, aktivitas, campaign, dan service context agar penjualan tidak kehilangan follow-up.

## Actors/Flow

Marketing captures lead -> assigns owner -> qualifies -> opportunity -> quotation/SO; sales logs calls/tasks; manager reviews pipeline; service consumes customer timeline. Duplicate party matching uses configurable fields and human merge approval.

## Validation and Workflow

Lead source, consent, owner, next activity, probability, expected close, and company scope required as applicable. Stages New/Qualified/Proposal/Negotiation/Won/Lost; lost requires reason. SLA reminder for overdue activity.

## Reports/Dashboard

Operational lead queue/activity overdue; management funnel, win rate, velocity, source ROI, customer profitability; executive pipeline forecast and retention. Pivot/export/PDF/print required.
