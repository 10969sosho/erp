# KPI

## Definitions

| KPI | Formula | Grain | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|
| Gross Margin % | (Revenue-COGS)/Revenue | period/item | Sales/Finance |
| DSO | AR / credit sales * days | company | Finance |
| DPO | AP / purchases * days | company | Finance |
| Fill Rate | delivered qty / ordered qty | order/period | Warehouse |
| Inventory Turnover | COGS / average inventory | period | Supply Chain |
| Stock Accuracy | matched count / counted lines | count session | Warehouse |
| OTIF | on-time-in-full deliveries / deliveries | supplier/customer | Purchasing/Warehouse |
| Quote Win Rate | won opportunities / closed opportunities | period | Sales |
| Approval Cycle | approved_at-submitted_at | entity | Process Owner |
| Purchase Price Variance | actual cost - reference cost | item/period | Purchasing |

## Governance

KPI formula versioned, timezone/currency explicit, exclusions documented, data freshness displayed, and changes require owner approval. KPI is not a substitute for source report reconciliation.
